### 1. EchoJay devices on a Link (your R4 and Test 5)

Three separate faults, all of them ours:

- **The Link never applied our devices' values.** `applyStagedSetParams` read `getParameters()` and
  dropped every frame with "that plugin has 0" — true of every EchoJay device, which has no juce
  parameters at all. It now goes through the device's own schema, by the same ordered list the
  publish half uses, so frame index *i* means the same control at both ends.
- **The gesture was only looked for when a frame arrived FROM the Link.** So a Link publishing
  nothing left your knobs dead *and* silent — the refusal road that would have said so was never
  reached. The poll runs on our own 30 Hz tick now.
- **With the switch ON, a device fell to the edit-here road** (your Test 5: nothing heard until you
  left the rack). An EchoJay device has no separate editor to embed — its view is our code either
  way — so it now takes the live copy road whatever the switch says, and the card says why.

And the publish half's silent `return` when a slot has nothing to publish now says so once per slot.

### 2. The switch, the tab click, and the watchdog

A user switch and a Chain-tab click on a rack that is not connected now do what reopening the window
does: let go of anything stranded in flight, apply and release the live session by the normal road
(your edits are not the price of a reset), re-take the one-ask-per-window baseline, and engage the
selected rack fresh. The window's spent ask was the reason switching away and back could not help.

The watchdog: three seconds in a connecting state, then that reset, at most twice, then

> Couldn't connect to <rack>. Close and reopen this window, or open that Link's own window to edit it there.

and the spinner stops. The apply flag also has a bound now (15 s): it was unbounded, and the engage
tick returns on it before it does anything else, so one stranded apply stalled every engage for the
life of the window.

### 3. Menus and popovers

`prepareForPopupOverGuest` returns what it took down, and the restore puts both halves back: the
pop-out is raised and **the inline editor is reopened on the slot it was on**. Hung on the one
callback each surface cannot skip — the picker's destructor, the menu's async result (which fires
with 0 on Esc and on a click outside), and, for the Placement popover, the death of its own content
component, since a CallOutBox in this JUCE has no dismissal callback. The V2's rack menu reopens on
a dismissal only: a pick replaces the rack, and reopening by index on a different rack is the "a
slot index is not a rack" fault.
