## 2. Why the A/B check missed it, and the better rule
Zip's one reading was at norm 0 (the fully open end: 0.00 → 0.00 under both policies), where every policy — and every
write — reads the same; and the check compares policies, not writes. Proposed rule, two parts: (a) the A/B reading is taken
at the tone check's own pick (the section-6 norm for g = 2 at L_ref), with the record's full write list, and (b) **any tone
check that reads within 0.3 dB of 0 where the curve predicts ≥ 1 dB is "write-sensitive": the run compares the check's
writes against the sweep's trace `set` lines and names the first that differs** — that comparison would have said "ratio:
sweep 0.4219, check 0.0000" on 5 Oct. A re-sweep follows only when the writes agree and the reading still differs.
