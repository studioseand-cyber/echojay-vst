================================================================================================
WHAT THIS BUILD IS
================================================================================================

The lease stops being an audio act, and the machinery that made it one is deleted.

Sean's complaint was a kick that left its own channel strip, its sends and its drum bus UNPROCESSED while
the processed copy was summed in at the V2's position. Stage 1 (10 Oct) turned that off behind named
predicates and left the paths in place, because "the detour's machinery is the only existing description of
what correct monitoring looked like, and deleting it in the same change that disables it would leave
nothing to compare a regression against". Stages 1-5 are green, so this is the deletion.

THE LINK SIDE
  rackLeaseEngage / rackLeaseRelease -> rackLeaseAddress(), with no counterpart: an engage no longer does
  anything a release has to undo. Gone with them: rackLeasePrior_ (and with it the persistent bypass after
  a hand-back, which came from a slot ADDED during a lease having no prior), leasePriorBypass_,
  rackLeaseMuteWant_, slotWetParkedSaid_ and the parked-rack arm, leaseBaseRev_ as a live field, and the
  prior remap through a structure plan. In ChainHost the whole bypass OVERLAY: setLeaseBypass,
  setAttachBypassed, attachBypassed_. setSlotBypassed is the one author now and intendedBypassed moves with
  it, so the bypass DISPLAY bug has nothing left to read.

  An older V2's muteOut is no longer merely disobeyed - it is not read. Selecting a Link cannot silence a
  channel by any route, and linksync_test proves it on rendered audio through a REAL lease file.

THE V2 SIDE
  The ring consume, borrowHost_->process, the stamp-based alignment, both AlignDelay lines, the
  16,384-sample budget, borrowCtxMix_, the injection, the in-context decision and its three banners, the
  mute closed loop, the honorary strip and the monitoring fallback.

  ADDRESSING A LINK NOW COSTS THE HOST NO PDC AT ALL. The budget was added to this plugin's reported
  latency whenever a capable Link was merely PRESENT in the session - 341.3 ms at 48 k, paid whether or not
  any rack was ever addressed. A project with twelve Links carried it for all twelve.

A BUILD IS A COMMAND
  The last V2 edit that was not. Every other structural edit had gone to the Link when it was made since
  20 September; a build landed in the mirror and waited for the rack to be left, on the stated grounds that
  "the Link refuses chain-cmds while leased" - a refusal that only ever applied to a sender that is NOT the
  lease holder, and the holder is who is building. Nothing a user builds lives only on the V2 side now, so
  the 4 October save fix's key (borrowedRacks) is no longer WRITTEN. Its READER stays, for the projects
  that still hold one.

================================================================================================
THE FINDING THIS ROUND TURNED UP, which is the one worth reading
================================================================================================

Leaving the ring consume running would not have been safe. The ring taps POST-RACK, and since stage 1 the
Link's own rack is no longer bypassed while addressed - so the V2's copy was being fed a channel this rack
had already processed, and processing it AGAIN.

Anything that MEASURED on that copy was measuring double processing, and the compressor calibration did:
calibTick advanced the loop on the borrowed host for a leased rack. Nobody could hear it, because stage 1
had already stopped anyone listening to that copy. It is the same shape as the GR unit bug found on 10
October - a number with nothing to contradict it.

The owner follows the audio now. The Link always measures and steps its own rack (calibTickOwnRack's lease
gate and startCalibFromBlock's refusal both go); the V2 renders the card from what that Link publishes,
which is what it already did after a deselect. calib_link_guard's 21t-g (6a) leg is inverted to match, and
still proves it on the ADDRESSING rather than on an empty rack so "started" cannot be a coincidence.

================================================================================================
IN-HOST TESTS THIS BUILD IS FOR
================================================================================================

1. THE KICK, which is the whole point. A Link on a kick, routed to a drum bus, selected in the V2. The
   kick should go through its fader, its sends and the drum bus exactly as it does unselected - and
   DESELECTING should change nothing audible either. The acceptance test from stage 1 is green on rendered
   audio; this is it with your ears and your routing.
2. SELECT AND DESELECT A LINK WHILE PLAYING. No volume jump, no PDC re-run, no silence, no click. The
   budget is gone, so the reported latency no longer changes when a Link appears or is addressed.
3. BUILD A CHAIN ON A LINK AND SAVE WITHOUT SWITCHING RACKS. Reopen. The rack should be there, once. This
   is the 4 October bug, and the build now reaches the Link when you make it rather than when you leave.
4. OPEN A PROJECT SAVED BY ship_2026-10-10b OR 09a that had a rack addressed. Nothing doubled, nothing
   lost. If that project was saved mid-lease its borrowed copy is still handed back, on the Link's own two
   revisions.
5. A COMPRESSOR CALIBRATION ON A LINK RACK. The figures are now measured by the Link, on the plugins in the
   audio path. Worth a look at whether the numbers are the ones you would expect, because until today they
   were measured on doubly-processed audio.
6. SOLO A LINK, addressed and not addressed. The sound should not change depending on whether it is
   selected - it is heard from its own ring either way now. (Whether solo should inject at all is a
   question in the next round's queue, deliberately not answered here.)

================================================================================================
DOWNGRADE SAFETY
================================================================================================

MECHANISED THIS ROUND, and that is new: downgrade_keys_guard (gated, 0.16 s) reads each reference build's
OWN SOURCE at its commit, takes the state keys it both READ and WROTE - that build's live contract with
itself - and fails if this tree no longer writes one without a recorded reason. The rule has been argued in
prose at the end of every round; a removal round is the worst one to rely on that.

It reports GREEN with exactly one deliberate removal, named and reasoned in the guard itself:
  borrowedRacks (and uid / baseRev / chain, its entry's fields). An older build finds the key ABSENT, which
  is the case it was always written to handle - it was additive from the start, present only while a borrow
  was open - and it needs nothing from it, because the Link's own chunk has the rack.

It also found, on its first pass, chainLoadedDescXml: a legacy READER kept for the single-plugin key from
before chainSlotsXml, which nothing has written for a long time. That is why the comparison is against the
old build's read+write contract and not just its reads.

The Link's own chunk: all 21 keys both reference builds read are still written. leaseBaseRev among them -
it is a PASS-THROUGH of whatever the chunk carried now, because writing a live -1 would make every older
V2's saved copy read "no borrow was open", which is the one value that DROPS it. That is Sean's 4 October
bug, reintroduced by a deletion, and it is the trap this migration was most likely to fall into.

WHAT THE GUARD CANNOT DO, said plainly: it does not run the old binary, so it proves the SHAPE is still
fed, not that the old build is happy with the VALUES. A key whose meaning changed while its name stayed
would pass.

WHAT THE GATE CAUGHT, AND IT WAS A REAL DEFECT
================================================================================================

calibTick both ADVANCES a loop and RENDERS its card, and I moved both when only advancing should have
moved: the early return for a Link uid handed back {} instead of loop.card(). On a Link rack mid-hunt the
card went blank. ui_guard's 21t-d wiring leg - "V2 renders the card from a loop the OTHER host is running"
- caught it within the hour. The fix is smaller than the defect: a Link rack takes the road a rack whose
slots have gone already took, so there is no second early return.

My own new assertion was wrong once too, in the instructive direction: "the card still comes back" read
"not empty", which is a claim about a fixture's measurements rather than about rendering. It went RED on a
loop that had legitimately measured nothing. It compares the tick against the state now.

Three legs asserted the old owner and are inverted, not loosened: calib_link_guard 21t-g (6a), ui_guard
21t-i, ui_guard's save-fix block. One fixture (ui_guard R2) was made deterministic: it read "4 edit(s) ...
(build, build, bypass, remove)" where it expected two, because a build is a command now and an earlier
leg's build leaves its own unacked push behind under an isolated home where nothing answers. The product
was telling the truth about a queue the leg did not mean to be measuring.

================================================================================================
DELIBERATELY NOT DONE
================================================================================================

THE BORROWED HOST AS AN OBJECT stays, as a non-authoritative mirror. Sean's ruling, 11 October, on a
recommendation: it is no longer an audio path - it neither plays nor measures and nothing is fed to it -
but it is still the V2 editor's READ MODEL for a Link rack (slot names, dial maps, master wet, loop state,
the EQ editors, which rack Save serialises). 119 direct uses and 15 test directories, about ten gated.
Deleting it is mechanical NOW in a way it was not a week ago, because nothing depends on that mirror being
authoritative any more: every edit to a Link rack is a command sent when it is made, so the mirror and the
Link agree by construction rather than by a hand-back. It is item 1 of the next round, and the one new
piece of behaviour in it is the plugin editor - open_editor exists and the Link honours it, but the V2's
card still opens its own copy's editor, which is why a hosted plugin's own settings are the one thing that
still commits at deselect.

THE SOLO PRESS still injects a Link's ring into the V2's output (through-main on a Mix/Master Bus main).
Not changed, deliberately: solo is a user-facing monitoring feature with its own spec and its own legs, and
quietly changing what a solo press does is the same class of act as the complaint that started all this.
What DID change is its source - a soloed Link is heard from its own ring whether or not it is addressed,
instead of sometimes from the V2's copy, which was the same channel processed twice. Whether it should
inject at all is a question for Sean in the next round's queue.
